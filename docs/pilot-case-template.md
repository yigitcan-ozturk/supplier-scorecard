## Confidentiality / sanitization

Before any public summary:

- replace supplier/customer/project identities;
- remove exact commercial values where they could reveal the case;
- remove contacts and contract references;
- remove proprietary drawings/specifications;
- report only aggregate timing, category-level and workflow findings.

## Pilot acceptance

A case counts toward Phase 3 only when:

- the baseline process is captured;
- the actual scorecard workflow is completed;
- a decision record is retained privately;
- human review outcome is captured;
- measurable impact or a clear no-value finding is recorded;
- product findings are classified rather than immediately converted into features.
